COMMON INQUIRIES

Transparency by Design.

Have questions about our process or technical standards? We believe in clear answers and zero friction.

Who actually owns the website code?

+

You do. Unlike agencies that use proprietary locked systems to keep you paying forever, we believe you should own your business assets. While most clients prefer us to handle technical hosting and maintenance, the ownership of the code always belongs to you.

Will the site work on slow rural connections?

+

Yes. This is a core development standard for our studio. By writing clean, manual code and optimizing every asset, we ensure your site loads quickly even in areas with limited connectivity or on older mobile devices.

Will my site work correctly on a smartphone?

+

Your site is built for mobile first. We recognize that your customers are often looking for your services while on a job site or on the road. We optimize the interface specifically for thumb navigation and fast loading on mobile data networks.

How long does a typical build take?

+

Our specialized workflow allows us to deliver high performance digital infrastructure in around 2 to 4 weeks. Provided the necessary project information and assets are available, we move from strategy to launch with absolute precision and zero unnecessary delays.

Can I update the content myself?

+

We provide the capability for you to handle basic text and image updates. However, most of our clients choose our Monthly Infrastructure Plan. This ensures that your site remains optimized, secure, and technically sound without you having to touch a single line of code.

Are there any hidden fees?

+

None. We believe in total transparency. Your initial quote covers the complete build of the site. You will be responsible for purchasing your own domain name to ensure you maintain full ownership of your digital address. While we offer professional management plans for security and updates, these are optional and clearly defined upfront.

Do you offer support after the launch?

+

Absolutely. We do not just hand over a link and disappear. We offer managed infrastructure plans to handle hosting, security updates, and performance monitoring. This professional management ensures your site stays stable and resistant to exploits.

How long will this website last?

+

Because we use clean, manual code rather than relying on trendy, bloated templates, our sites have a significantly longer lifespan. We build on a foundation of technical authority, meaning your site is engineered to remain fast and secure for years to come.
