COMMON INQUIRIES

Transparency by Design.

Have questions about our process or technical standards? We believe in clear answers and zero friction.

Who actually owns the website code?

+

You do. Unlike agencies that use proprietary locked systems to keep you paying forever, we believe you should own your business assets. While most clients prefer us to handle technical hosting and maintenance, the keys to the kingdom always belong to you.

Will the site work on slow rural connections?

+

Yes. This is a core development standard for us. By writing clean, manual code and optimizing every asset, we ensure your site loads quickly even in areas with limited connectivity or on older mobile devices.

How long does a typical build take?

+

Because we prioritize precision over speed-running templates, a professional build typically takes 4 to 6 weeks. This timeline covers everything from initial strategy and blueprinting to development and our final precision audit.

Can I update the content myself?

+

We build with your workflow in mind. During the blueprinting phase, we discuss how often you need to change content. We can set up specialized areas for you to update text and photos easily without needing to touch the underlying code.

Do you offer technical support after launch?

+

Absolutely. We don't just hand over a link and disappear. We offer various managed infrastructure plans to handle hosting, security updates, and performance monitoring so you can stay focused on running your business.
